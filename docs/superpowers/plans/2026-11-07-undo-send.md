# Undo send Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking. Build test-first.

**Goal:** Hold every sent email in the main process for the "Undo send" delay (default 10 s), with
a countdown bar and Undo in the window, and follow "If you close the app during the countdown" on
quit and after a kill, never sending twice.

**Spec:** [docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-07-undo-send-design.md](../specs/2026-11-07-undo-send-design.md).
Also binding: roadmap Constraints and Anti-goals, `AGENTS.md`.

### 2. Main: the `UndoSend` class (unit tests with fake timers, fake host)

Tests first in `tests/undo-send.test.ts`:

- [ ] sends after the delay, not before; Off sends immediately and stores nothing;
- [ ] undo before the delay sends nothing, removes the record and returns the draft;
- [ ] undo racing the deadline: once the timer has fired, undo is "too late" and it sends once;
- [ ] the record is durable before the timer starts, and `sending` before submission;
- [ ] quit, "Send it anyway": sends waiting ones at once, the returned promise settles after;
- [ ] quit, "Keep it as a draft": sends nothing, saves the drafts to the server, drops records;
- [ ] relaunch after a kill: past due + send → sends exactly once after the account connects;
      not yet due → countdown resumes; keep → nothing sent; `sending`/journal started → dropped;
- [ ] draft edited or deleted meanwhile → not sent, with a reason;
- [ ] sending the same draft again replaces its countdown;
- [ ] several pending sends are summarised in due order.

Then `apps/desktop/src/main/undo-send.ts`.
